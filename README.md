ChargeAfter is an embedded lending and point-of-sale financing platform that connects merchants to an independent network of 40+ lenders, returning personalized consumer financing offers at checkout across online, in-store, and call-center channels. Merchants integrate through a client-side JavaScript SDK (Apply, Checkout, and promotional widgets), through ecommerce plugins for Shopify, Magento 2, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, NopCommerce, Shopware and Miva, or directly against a bearer-authenticated REST API that covers sessions and offers, application status, accounts, charges (settle, refund, void, delivery), consumer lookup, funding reconciliation reports, omni-link generation, and lender dispute management. The merchant back office is Operate, and event notifications are delivered as webhooks.

**Areas:** Financial Services · Payments · Lending · Buy Now Pay Later · Embedded Finance · Point-of-Sale · E-Commerce · Checkout · Consumer Credit · Retail
